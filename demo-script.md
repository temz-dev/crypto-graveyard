# Crypto Graveyard — Demo Video Script

Kiroween 2026, Costume Contest. Target length 2:30–2:45, under 3 minutes.
Voice: calm male "Measured Sundial" (`avocado_v2:qvd_00325`).
Captions: burned in, FontSize 15, MarginV 12 (small, clear of the UI).
Footage: real Playwright screen recordings of the app — no faked UI.

## Honesty note

This machine's sandbox proxy blocks the CoinGecko and alternative.me APIs
inside Chromium, so the recordings show the app's labelled offline snapshot
(the same figures, dated). The narration describes the live behaviour and
the offline fallback as a feature, and notes live data loads on a normal
connection. No live data is faked.

## Sections

### S1 — Hook (hero)

> Every market has its winners. Crypto has its graveyard. Coins that
> promised the moon, then vanished — taking billions with them. This is
> Crypto Graveyard: live prices, haunted sentiment, and the coins that
> didn't make it.

Footage: hero with moon and fog, slow scroll down.

### S2 — Live prices

> First, the living. The top ten coins by market cap, pulled live from
> CoinGecko — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and the rest, green when they're
> up, blood-red when they're down. And if the connection drops, the table
> falls back to a dated offline snapshot, clearly labelled, so you always
> know what you're looking at.

Footage: prices table, hold on Bitcoin row and the change colours.

### S3 — Fear & Greed gauge

> Next, the mood of the market. The Fear and Greed meter, from
> alternative dot me. Right now it reads thirteen — Extreme Fear. The
> needle swings from panic on the left to euphoria on the right, so you
> can see at a glance whether the market is terrified, or greedy.

Footage: gauge section, needle at 13 / Extreme Fear.

### S4 — The Graveyard

> And then, the graveyard. Six coins that died, each with a real story.
> BitConnect — peaked at four hundred and sixty-three dollars, promised
> forty percent a month, and took roughly two point four billion dollars
> with it. A Ponzi scheme. Squid Game Token — up seventy-five thousand
> percent in days, then the developers drained three point three eight
> million dollars in under five minutes, because holders couldn't sell.
> Terra Luna — a forty-billion-dollar collapse in days, when its
> stablecoin lost its peg. Click any stone to unearth the full story —
> and the lesson it teaches.

Footage: graveyard grid; click BitConnect, then Squid Game Token, then
Terra Luna — each expands to show its story and lesson.

### S5 — Watchlist

> Want to keep an eye on the living? Tap Watch on any coin and it joins
> your watchlist — saved in your browser, so it's still there when you
> come back. No account, no sign-up. Just the coins you care about, and
> the ghosts you don't want to join.

Footage: click + Watch on Bitcoin, scroll to watchlist, row appears.

### S6 — Built with Kiro / close

> This whole thing was built with Kiro — Amazon's AI coding tool —

> Contest: a spooky design that makes the app better, not just darker.
> Crypto Graveyard — check the prices, mind the mood, and learn from
> the dead.

Footage: slow scroll back to the hero, hold on the title and moon.
