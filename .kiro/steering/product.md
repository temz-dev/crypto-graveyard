# Product Steering — Crypto Graveyard

## What this is

A haunted crypto market dashboard for the Kiroween 2026 Costume Contest. The user is a crypto holder or learner who wants live prices, a fear/greed read, and the lessons of dead coins — in one place, with a spooky design that does real work.

## Product rules

- The graveyard is the lesson, not decoration: every tombstone teaches one failure pattern.
- Never invent a price, an index value, or a death fact. Live data comes from public APIs; anything static carries a source note and a date.
- When live data is unavailable, say so in the UI. A stale number presented as live is a bug, not a fallback.
- Educational only. The footer says so. No financial advice anywhere.
- No face, no voice, no copyrighted material in the product or its demo.

## Voice

Plain English, dry humour, no hype. "RIP" dates and causes of death are stated factually. The joke is the theme; the numbers are serious.
